Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tool Genie completely free to use?

Yes. You can generate unlimited flashcards from lecture notes, PDFs, and YouTube transcripts, edit cards directly, study in flip mode, and export RFC-compliant CSVs for Anki with no paywall.

How do I import the exported CSV into Anki?

In Anki, click File → Import, select the downloaded .csv file, set Field 1 to Front and Field 2 to Back, ensure separator is set to Comma (or Tab if copied), and click Import.

Do you store or sell my uploaded study notes or PDFs?

No. PDF text extraction is processed directly on your device in the browser using Mozilla PDF.js. Your notes are never sold, retained for training, or shared with third parties.
