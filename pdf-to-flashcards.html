FAQ

Can I upload a PDF file?

Yes! Drag and drop any PDF file directly into the dropzone or click to browse. Tool Genie parses the text page-by-page directly in your browser and drafts your cards instantly.

Are my PDF cards editable?

Yes. Every single question and answer card is directly editable in place, and you can delete unwanted cards or add custom cards before exporting to Anki.

How do I import these into Anki?

Click "Export CSV for Anki" or "Copy for Anki". In Anki, click File → Import and choose the downloaded file, mapping field 1 to Front and field 2 to Back.
